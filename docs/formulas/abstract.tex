PubChem ($\sim$123M SMILES) and ZINC20 ($\sim$1G SMILES) records are turned into sparse counted ECFP rows. At preprocessing time, each positive optionally seeds one or more MutatorMix negatives carrying a multi-label violation bitset. Positives and negatives share the same encoder, and the decoder is trained to reconstruct the source positive in either case (denoising / correction framing).
